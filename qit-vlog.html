Self-Directed Doctoral Qualifying Curriculum

The 5-Stage Information-Theoretic Spine

Every module bridges classical computer science and Shannon theory directly into quantum computation and quantum information theory.

Stage I — Classical Computation, Reversible Logic & Shannon Information Theory ACTIVE VOLUME • 3 VLOGS FILMED
CLASSICAL CORE

Turing machines, Boolean circuit complexity, Landauer's erasure bound ($k_B T \ln 2$), Toffoli/Fredkin reversible logic, Shannon entropy $H(X)$, Kullback-Leibler divergence $D(p\parallel q)$, Noisy Channel Coding Theorem.

QUANTUM INFORMATION BRIDGE

Why unitary quantum evolution demands reversibility; Bennett's uncomputation trick to clear ancilla garbage before interference; $H(X)$ as the diagonal limit of Von Neumann entropy $S(\rho)$.

PRIMARY MONOGRAPHS

Elements of Information Theory (Cover & Thomas); Lectures on Computation (Feynman); Information Theory, Inference, and Learning Algorithms (MacKay).

Stage II — Finite-Dimensional Hilbert Spaces, Quantum Postulates & Non-Local Games IN RECORDING • 1 VLOG FILMED
MATHEMATICAL SUBSTRATE

Spectral Theorem for normal operators, Tensor products $\mathcal{H}_A \otimes \mathcal{H}_B$, Singular Value & Schmidt Decomposition, Projective vs. POVM measurements.

INFORMATION-THEORETIC FOCUS

No-Cloning & No-Signaling duality; Bell inequalities framed as the CHSH cooperative information game (classical $75\%$ vs. Tsirelson $\cos^2(\pi/8) \approx 85.4\%$ quantum limit).

PRIMARY MONOGRAPHS

Quantum Computation and Quantum Information (Nielsen & Chuang); The Theory of Quantum Information (Watrous).

Stage III — Quantum Circuits, Oracular Complexity & Algorithmic Speedups UPCOMING VOLUME
ALGORITHMIC SPINE

Universal gate sets, Solovay-Kitaev theorem, Phase kickback, Deutsch-Jozsa, Bernstein-Vazirani, Simon's periodicity, QFT, Shor's factoring, Grover's $\mathcal{O}(\sqrt{N})$ optimality proof.

INFORMATION-THEORETIC FOCUS

Query complexity lower bounds via the hybrid/adversary method; how quantum interference concentrates probability mass without violating Holevo's bound.

PRIMARY MONOGRAPHS

Quantum Computing Since Democritus (Aaronson); Classical and Quantum Computation (Kitaev, Shen & Vyalyi).

Stage IV — Quantum Information Theory: Channels, Entropy & Entanglement Resource Theory CORE DOCTORAL CONCENTRATION
OPEN SYSTEMS & ENTROPY

Density operators $\rho$, Purifications, CPTP Quantum Channels, Kraus & Stinespring Dilation, Von Neumann Entropy $S(\rho) = -\operatorname{Tr}(\rho \log_2 \rho)$, Strong Subadditivity.

CAPACITIES & ENTANGLEMENT

Accessible information & Holevo's Bound $\chi$, Schumacher quantum source coding, HSW classical capacity, Quantum capacity $Q$, LOCC & Entanglement Distillation.

PRIMARY MONOGRAPHS

Quantum Information Theory (Mark Wilde); The Theory of Quantum Information (John Watrous); Preskill Lecture Notes.

Stage V — Quantum Error Correction, Complexity Theory (BQP / QMA) & Thesis Monograph CAPSTONE STAGE
FAULT TOLERANCE

Pauli Stabilizer Formalism, Gottesman-Knill Theorem, Shor 9-qubit & Steane 7-qubit codes, Topological Surface Codes, Fault-Tolerant Threshold Theorem.

QUANTUM COMPLEXITY

$\text{BQP}$ vs $\text{PP}$, $\text{QMA}$-Completeness of the $k$-Local Hamiltonian Problem (quantum Cook-Levin), Device-Independent QKD.

PRIMARY MONOGRAPHS

Stabilizer Codes and Quantum Error Correction (Gottesman PhD Thesis); Quantum Hamiltonian Complexity (Gharibian et al.).
